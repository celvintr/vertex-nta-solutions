Good to know

Are estimates really free?+

Yes. We visit, measure and send a written, no-obligation estimate at no cost.

Are you licensed and insured?+

Yes — fully licensed and insured. Documentation is available on request.

What areas do you serve?+

All of Honduras — from San Pedro Sula, La Ceiba and the north coast to Tegucigalpa, Comayagua and the south. Ask us about your town.

What materials do you use?+

Recognized brands: aluzinc and metal sheet for roofing, ceramic and porcelain for floors, PVC/CPVC for plumbing, and drywall/gypsum for ceilings and partitions. We advise you on the best option for your budget.
